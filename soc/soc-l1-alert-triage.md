# SOC L1 Alert Triage

## Overview

This TryHackMe room focused on understanding security alerts and developing a structured approach for SOC Level 1 alert triage.

## Key Topics

* SOC alerts
* Alert triage
* Security event investigation
* Prioritization
* False positives
* Indicators of compromise
* Escalation

## Alert Triage Process

```text
Receive Alert
     ↓
Validate Alert
     ↓
Collect Evidence
     ↓
Investigate Activity
     ↓
Identify Indicators
     ↓
Determine Severity
     ↓
Close or Escalate
```

## What I Learned

I learned that effective alert triage requires analysts to validate alerts, gather relevant evidence, determine whether activity is malicious, and prioritize incidents based on risk and impact.
