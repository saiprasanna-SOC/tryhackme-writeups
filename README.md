## Investigation Methodology

For security-related investigations, I use a structured approach:

```text
Identify Alert / Evidence
        ↓
Initial Triage
        ↓
Collect Evidence
        ↓
Analyze Artifacts
        ↓
Identify IOCs
        ↓
Use Threat Intelligence
        ↓
Determine Impact
        ↓
Document Findings
        ↓
Recommend Response
```
